# Knowledge base audit — Republic of Nature
**3 August 2026. 67 active chunks reviewed, one by one.**

Written after a real buyer (Vikram, 919884032506) was told the project is *"an 8 acre
community of 204 homes"*, then two messages later *"three phases — about 3 acres, 4.8
and 11.5"*, and was offered *"villas, apartments and villaments"* — a product we do
not sell.

**The bot did not invent any of that.** It quoted the knowledge base accurately. Every
safety guard passed, because a real source was cited. The knowledge base itself
disagrees with itself, and no amount of code can fix that.

### The single most important finding

**The FAQ is an internal engineering review sheet, not customer-facing material.**

Roughly two-thirds of it is FSI, OSR areas, WTP/STP locations, isolated footings,
300 mm wall thicknesses, bioswale depth, basement waterproofing, corridor widths.

This explains something we blamed on the bot for two days. We kept telling it *"lead
with what it is like to live there, not what is installed there"* — but what is
installed there is most of what it has to read. **The tone problem is a corpus
problem.** No prompt fixes a library that contains mostly plumbing.

### And a pattern, not a single chunk
The corpus answers **eleven** buyer questions with a bare **"No"**: pools, piped gas,
water meter, intercom, glass, lease guarantee, sprinklers, countertop, apartment
swimming pool, and more.

A premium bot saying "No" eleven different ways is corrosive. Each of these needs
either a positive framing or a rule to escalate instead.

## D. What is missing that buyers actually ask

- **No amenities answer.** The clubhouse (60,000 sqft), mini theatre, community hall,
  lagoon, bioswale, convenience store, EV charging and solar are scattered across
  eight chunks and never assembled. *"What are the amenities?"* — one of the commonest
  questions — returns fragments.
- **No possession or handover date.** Correct that it is absent, but it means every
  such question goes to a human. It is among the top three questions asked.
- **No RERA number.**
- **No developer story.** Who is building this, and what have they built before?
  Premium buyers ask this early and we have nothing.
- **No schools, hospitals, offices or metro** (chunk 356 says so explicitly).
  Accepted earlier as fine for a weekend-home product — but it is the single largest
  escalation category.
- **Nothing saying the GTB Carnival is over.** People are still arriving asking to
  attend it, three weeks after it ended.

## What I recommend doing immediately, before any answers arrive

1. **Stop new outreach** — no new buyers pulled in while the corpus is wrong.
2. **Quarantine the dangerous chunks** — 391 (204/8 acres), the seven Phase 3 chunks,
   407 (flood), 365 (internal instruction), 414 (fire safety). Reversible in minutes.
   The bot then says *"let me have someone confirm"* instead of stating something
   false.
3. **Keep replying to people already in conversation.** A full pause also stops
   replies, and going silent on someone mid-conversation is the defect we spent
   2 August removing.

That combination removes the wrong facts at source without ghosting anyone.
