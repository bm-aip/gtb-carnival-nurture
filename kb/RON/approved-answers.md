# RON — approved answers

**Source: the marketing team's replies to `RON-QUESTIONS-FOR-MARKETING.md`, returned
5 August 2026.** Every answer below was written or confirmed by marketing. This
document outranks `faqs.md`: where the two disagree, the FAQ chunk is quarantined and
this file is the answer.

Do not edit an answer here to make it read better. If wording is wrong, it goes back
to marketing and comes back changed — the point of this file is that a buyer sees the
sentence the business approved, not a sentence we assembled.

## How big is Republic of Nature? How many acres?

Republic of Nature is a 32-acre community on ECR, near Kovalam Junction.

## How many homes are there in total? How many units?

There are 343 homes in total across the community.

## Is there a swimming pool? Do apartment buyers get a pool?

Yes. There is a common swimming pool for residents. Apartments do not have a
separate pool of their own — the pool is a shared community amenity.

<!-- approved:Q5 -->

## Is there centralised air-conditioning?

The homes are not centrally air-conditioned. The common areas are — the clubhouse,
the theatre and similar shared spaces.

<!-- approved:Q6 -->

## How many clubhouses are there and how big is the clubhouse?

There is one world-class clubhouse of over 1,00,000 sqft.

<!-- approved:Q7 -->

## What are the amenities?

Republic of Nature offers an extensive range of resort-style amenities designed
around recreation, wellness, nature and convenient living. Residents can enjoy
swimming pools, a clubhouse, gym and fitness facilities, multiple sports courts,
jogging and cycling tracks, a mini theatre, spa, sauna, yoga and meditation spaces,
landscaped gardens, water features, an amphitheatre and a birdwatching deck. The
community also provides managed-living services such as an AI concierge, housekeeping,
chef dining, chauffeur services, EV charging, medical support, physiotherapy, pharmacy
access and organic produce delivery.

No flooding has occurred in this area to date. The homes sit a metre above road level,
and the site has storm-water drainage provision throughout.

## Rules that travel with these answers

Everything below this line is addressed to the assistant, not to a buyer. The
ingester excludes it from the corpus as content; the rules are attached to the chunks
they govern.

| Rule | Attached to |

## What this file deliberately does NOT answer, and why

All three follow-up questions came back on 5 August. Nothing here is open — these are
decisions, and the assistant is built to them.

**Utility and specification questions go to sales.** Piped gas, water and power meters,
intercom, balcony glass, kitchen countertop, high-tension lines, fire safety equipment,
lease guarantee. The old FAQ answered each with a bare "No". Those chunks are
withdrawn, so the assistant has nothing to cite and defers — warmly, and by name. It
must never simply say no, and it must never say yes. See the deferral wording in
`content/answering-rules.md`.

Fire safety sits in this group deliberately. Marketing's answer and the FAQ contradict
each other, and it is the one item where being wrong is a safety matter rather than a
comfort one. A person answers it.

**Maintenance questions go to sales**, because the only answers we hold name the
provider and marketing said not to.

**The 1BHK is out of the plan** (marketing, 5 August). It stays off-category, so the
entry price remains the compact 2BHK at Rs 1.28 Cr and nothing about who reaches the
sales team changes.
